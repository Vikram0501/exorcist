TEEN GIRL VANISHES FROM VALE HOME

A folded clipping, damp from the porch, is marked with a child's initials. Read it carefully before logging the evidence.

Neighbours describe footsteps upstairs and moving lights before Evelyn vanished.

Sixteen-year-old Evelyn Vale vanished from her family home sometime during the night.

In the weeks before her disappearance, neighbours reported footsteps from the upper floor and lights moving through rooms long after the family had gone to bed. One resident said Evelyn had recently claimed that someone kept walking the house.

By morning she was gone. No trace of Evelyn has ever been found.
