VALE GIRL STILL MISSING

A folded clipping, damp from the porch, is marked with a child's initials. Read it carefully before logging the evidence.

Police renew appeal after neighbours report shouting at the isolated Vale property.

Authorities have renewed their search for sixteen-year-old Evelyn Vale, who vanished from her family home on Old Mill Road last month.

Her father, Daniel Vale, told police that Evelyn left during the night. Several neighbours, however, reported hearing a heated argument and a young girl crying from inside the house.

Investigators found no sign of Evelyn on the surrounding roads or woodland. Anyone with information is urged to contact the Vale County Sheriff's Office.
