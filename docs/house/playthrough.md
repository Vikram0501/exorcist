# Vale Manor: playable route

The player is an exorcist investigating Evelyn Vale's disappearance. The
hostile female ghost is Elias Wren; Evelyn appears separately after the rite.
The original scene is `source-assets/house.glb`; the game loads
the smaller `house-runtime.glb` generated by `python scripts/optimize-house-glb.py`.

1. Start on the front road and inspect the Canva newspaper on the porch. It
   reports that sixteen-year-old Evelyn vanished after neighbours heard
   footsteps upstairs and saw lights moving after bedtime.
2. Enter the house and inspect the family portrait. Evelyn's face is scratched
   out, unlike her photograph in the newspaper. Closing the inspection starts
   the upstairs main-bedroom telephone ringing after three seconds.
3. Answer that telephone. Evelyn says the voice upstairs sounds like her dead
   mother Margaret and directs the exorcist to her diary.
4. Inspect the diary beside Evelyn's bed, the music box in the main bedroom,
   and the scratches in the upstairs annex. The music box needs no key. The
   diary describes the wrong-sounding imitation of Margaret; the scratches
   show that Daniel locked Evelyn in and the spirit came through anyway.
5. Return to the kitchen and inspect the Vale estate service record. It names
   Elias Wren, a caretaker who died on the property in 1931. The four clues
   can be found in any order after answering the phone.
6. Open Daniel's envelope in the kitchen. It stays in place and his separate
   letter appears beside it for inspection. It reveals that Daniel found Evelyn dead in
   the annex, buried her in the unmarked backyard grave, concealed her death,
   and scratched out her portrait. Closing the letter triggers a brief
   close-up scare and then Elias's pursuit.
7. Sprint to Evelyn's grave behind the house. The grave area is safe. If Elias
   catches you, a scare plays before you can respawn at the front of the house;
   gathered evidence remains intact.
8. Face the grave and press `E`. The rite has five questions about Margaret's
   missed final note, the music-box melody, the locked annex, Daniel's damaged
   portrait, and Elias Wren's name. A wrong answer triggers a scare and a
   respawn, restarting the rite. The field notes can be opened during the rite
   while the game remains paused; stepping away retains the current rite step.
9. Elias is banished. Evelyn appears at the grave, thanks the exorcist, then
   fades. The Level 1 completion screen offers a replay.

`E` interacts, `I` opens field notes, `T` toggles the torch, `V` switches view, `Shift` sprints,
and `Esc` opens the case menu. The menu's **Controls** button shows the full
list. Story and movement pause during inspections, field notes, the rite, and
the menu. `R` respawns without clearing evidence; browser reload starts a
new case.

The story code and full readable evidence copy live in `src/levels/house/`.
The diary remains beside Evelyn's bed. Its authored `Diary Entry` page stays
hidden in the room and appears only in the inspection view; the same text is
transcribed in the inspection panel and field notes. The book can be revisited.
Every inspected object stays in the scene. Press `E` again to revisit it; the
newspaper and portrait can also be reopened. The envelope and revealed letter
both remain in the kitchen. `I` opens one field-notes list containing the
newspaper, portrait, phone call, all five later clues, the grave, and the rite
as they are discovered. No physical clue is carried to the grave.
The annex scratches are authored in the house scene. The fourth place setting is no
longer an investigation interaction. Run `npm test` and `npm run build` for
automated verification; a manual visual playthrough should still check prop
reachability, the chase route, and the grave scene.
