# Level 1 — Haunted House Full Game Walkthrough

## Core Concept

Level 1 is designed as a **single connected mystery**. Every room and exterior area should serve a purpose in the investigation, and the haunting should escalate in clear stages.

The overall gameplay progression is:

**Curiosity → Unease → Evidence → Paranormal Activity → Understanding → Danger → Exorcism**

The level should take approximately **35–50 minutes** on a first playthrough.

For now, the story uses a temporary character concept:

> A young woman named **Evelyn** was imprisoned and killed in the house by her father after he became obsessed with occult practices.

This story can be changed later. The important purpose of this document is to establish the **gameplay structure and sequence of events**.

---

# Full Walkthrough

## 1. Opening Cinematic — Arrival

The player is an **exorcist** sent to investigate reports of paranormal activity at an abandoned property.

A short introduction establishes the objective:

- Determine what is haunting the property.
- Discover why the spirit remains there.
- Identify what is binding the spirit to the house.
- Perform the correct exorcism.

The player begins in the **front yard** at night.

Starting equipment:

- Flashlight
- Exorcist's journal/notebook
- Basic exorcism equipment

The house is dark except for **one upstairs window with a light on**.

---

## 2. Crossing the Front Yard

The player walks toward the house.

Nothing attacks them.

Atmosphere should come from:

- Wind
- Trees
- Distant creaking
- Far-away animal sounds
- Subtle house noises

The **barn/shed** is visible but initially inaccessible.

As the player reaches the porch:

**The upstairs light switches off.**

---

## 3. Front Porch

The porch introduces basic interaction.

The player tries the front door.

It is unlocked.

The door slowly swings inward.

There should be **no major jumpscare here**.

The goal is to make entering the house feel voluntary and uncomfortable.

---

## 4. Fireplace Room — First Investigation Area

The player enters the fireplace/living room.

The front door closes behind them.

However, the door is **not locked yet**.

The room introduces object inspection.

Possible environmental objects:

- Family photographs
- Furniture
- Books
- Fireplace
- Old clock
- Personal belongings

The player discovers a family photograph showing:

- Father
- Mother
- Daughter

The daughter's face is damaged, scratched out, or obscured.

---

## 5. First Clue — The Family

Examining the photograph creates a journal entry.

Example:

**The Vale Family — 1987**

The player now understands that photographs, letters, and objects can provide important evidence.

---

## 6. First Paranormal Event

After examining the photograph:

**Footsteps slowly cross the upstairs floor.**

Five or six clear footsteps.

Then silence.

No ghost appears.

The player now knows that something may be upstairs.

---

## 7. Dining Room Investigation

The dining room contains more environmental clues.

The dining table may strangely be set for only **two people**, even though the photograph showed three family members.

The player finds:

- Newspaper clipping
- Letter
- Missing-person notice
- Family document

The player learns that **Evelyn disappeared**.

The official explanation was that she ran away.

This creates the first major contradiction.

---

## 8. Kitchen Investigation

The kitchen contains another clue.

Possible evidence:

- Medication
- Calendar
- Handwritten note
- Mother's letter
- Family schedule

The player learns that Evelyn's disappearance was not sudden.

Something had been happening inside the family for several weeks beforehand.

The kitchen also contains the **back door**.

Initially, the back door is:

- Jammed
- Locked
- Blocked
- Otherwise inaccessible

---

## 9. First Visual Ghost Appearance

When the player returns toward the fireplace room:

A woman is briefly visible **standing at the top of the stairs**.

When the player turns the flashlight toward her:

**She disappears.**

No screaming or loud jumpscare is needed.

---

## 10. Going Upstairs

The player now explores upstairs.

The atmosphere becomes:

- Darker
- Quieter
- More enclosed
- More threatening

Several doors are available.

The player should feel that they have some freedom to explore.

---

## 11. Small Bedroom — Evelyn's History

The smaller bedroom belonged to Evelyn.

The player discovers:

- Photographs
- Drawings
- Diary pages
- Letters
- Personal objects

The player learns that Evelyn had become increasingly afraid of her father.

She repeatedly mentions:

**"The shed."**

This makes the outdoor barn/shed important to the mystery.

---

## 12. Bathroom Paranormal Puzzle

The bathroom initially appears relatively normal.

The player interacts with:

- Mirror
- Tap
- Shower
- Sink

Condensation, blood, or supernatural writing appears on the mirror.

Possible message:

**HE LOCKED ME IN**

The bathroom door suddenly closes.

The lights flicker.

When the player escapes:

Someone or something runs down the hallway.

---

## 13. Main Bedroom — The Father's Secret

The main bedroom belonged to Evelyn's parents.

The player discovers evidence that the father became obsessed with:

- Spiritualism
- Occult practices
- Rituals
- Communicating with spirits
- Removing supposed possession

Possible evidence:

- Books
- Symbols
- Letters
- Research notes
- Ritual diagrams

The player begins to realize that the haunting did not start randomly.

---

## 14. Closet Puzzle

Inside the bedroom closet, the player discovers:

- Scratch marks
- Hidden panel
- Old key
- Moved furniture
- Evidence of concealed construction

The player realizes that the closet or nearby furniture had been used to hide the entrance to the **small square room**.

---

## 15. Small Square Room

The player gains access to the small hidden room.

This should be one of the strongest horror locations in the level.

The room contains very little.

Possible details:

- Old mattress
- Chain
- Chair
- Tally marks
- Scratches around the inside of the door
- Water bowl
- Torn fabric

It becomes obvious that:

**Someone had been kept prisoner here.**

---

## 16. Major Story Reveal

The player discovers Evelyn's final diary page, letter, or message.

The player learns:

- Her father believed something supernatural was attached to her.
- He imprisoned her in the square room.
- He attempted to "cleanse" or exorcise her.
- Evelyn insisted that she was never possessed.
- She was terrified of him.

---

## 17. First Proper Ghost Encounter

While examining the final clue:

The player hears breathing behind them.

They turn around.

Evelyn is standing in the doorway.

Instead of attacking, she quietly says:

**"Find him."**

She disappears.

Immediately afterward:

**All upstairs doors slam shut simultaneously.**

---

## 18. Back Door Opens

The player returns downstairs.

The house has subtly changed.

Possible differences:

- Furniture shifted
- Objects moved
- Lights changed
- Fireplace different
- Photographs altered

The front door can no longer be opened.

However:

**The kitchen back door is now standing open.**

The game naturally directs the player toward the backyard.

---

## 19. Backyard Investigation

The player exits into the backyard.

Possible trail elements:

- Footprints
- Drag marks
- Disturbed soil
- Torn diary pages
- Dropped objects
- Lantern light

The trail leads toward the barn/shed.

The road beyond the backyard is clearly visible.

---

## 20. Road Scare

The road should function primarily as atmosphere and a level boundary.

The player hears a vehicle approaching.

Headlights appear in the distance.

For a moment, it looks like someone may be arriving.

The headlights stop.

A human silhouette is standing in the road.

When the player shines the flashlight toward it:

**The figure disappears.**

The headlights vanish.

The road remains inaccessible.

---

## 21. Barn/Shed Opens

The barn that was previously inaccessible can now be entered.

Inside, the player finds the father's hidden workspace.

Possible objects:

- Ritual equipment
- Symbols
- Photographs
- Written notes
- Tools
- Strange containers
- Spiritual diagrams

The player discovers that the father had been experimenting with ways to:

- Contact spirits
- Control spirits
- Bind spirits
- Remove supposed possession

---

## 22. Discovering How Evelyn Died

The player finds evidence revealing Evelyn's true fate.

The exact cause of death can be finalized later.

Possible options:

- Strangulation
- Smoke inhalation
- Poisoning
- Ritual accident
- Physical restraint
- Failed exorcism

The important revelation is:

**Evelyn never ran away.**

The family covered up her death.

---

## 23. Discovering the Anchor

The player discovers an object binding Evelyn's spirit to the property.

Possible anchor objects:

- Necklace
- Photograph
- Lock of hair
- Bone fragment
- Childhood object
- Ritual object

The father deliberately used the object to bind Evelyn after her death.

The player takes the anchor.

---

## 24. First Chase Begins

Taking the anchor destabilizes the haunting.

The barn lights go out.

Something hits the exterior wall.

The player hears screaming from the house.

A hostile supernatural manifestation begins pursuing the player.

This is the first true chase sequence.

---

## 25. Run Back to the House

The player runs through the backyard toward the house.

The player cannot fight the entity.

Possible survival mechanics:

- Sprinting
- Closing doors
- Hiding briefly
- Avoiding obstacles
- Keeping distance

The barn and backyard, which previously felt relatively safe, are now dangerous.

---

## 26. The House Has Changed

The player re-enters the house.

The environment is now far more hostile.

Possible changes:

- Flickering lights
- Doors slamming automatically
- Moving furniture
- Loud whispers
- Objects falling
- Distorted photographs
- Fireplace igniting by itself
- Stronger ghost manifestations

Taking the anchor has destabilized the entire property.

---

## 27. Fireplace Revelation

The anchor reacts near the fireplace.

The player discovers hidden or partially burned documents inside or near it.

These reveal:

- Evelyn's full identity
- The father's actions
- The real circumstances surrounding her death
- Evidence of the cover-up

This completes another major part of the investigation.

---

## 28. Journal Assembles the Mystery

The journal now contains the three essential pieces of information needed for the exorcism:

### Identity
Who is the spirit?

### Death
How did the spirit die?

### Anchor
What object is binding the spirit to the property?

Optional evidence can provide additional story detail.

---

## 29. Large Empty Room Revealed as Ritual Room

The large upstairs empty room finally becomes important.

Earlier it appeared to be simply an unsettling empty space.

The player now discovers that the father once used the room for rituals.

Possible details:

- Symbols under carpet
- Burn marks
- Scratched floorboards
- Hidden ritual circle
- Candle positions
- Wall markings

This becomes the location for the final exorcism.

---

## 30. Exorcism Preparation

The player prepares the ritual.

Possible actions:

- Place the anchor in the centre
- Place ritual objects around the room
- Draw or activate a circle
- Light candles
- Position religious/spiritual items
- Open the exorcist's journal

This creates a short calm period before the finale.

---

## 31. Exorcism Begins

Once the ritual begins, the player cannot simply leave.

The house reacts violently.

Possible events:

- Doors slam
- Windows shake
- Lights break
- Furniture moves
- Screams come from downstairs
- Ghost manifestations surround the room

The player must continue performing the ritual.

---

## 32. The Ghost Attempts to Deceive the Player

Evelyn appears outside the ritual circle.

She may:

- Beg the player to stop
- Pretend to be innocent
- Become aggressive
- Change appearance
- Mimic other voices

The player must rely on what they discovered during the investigation.

The exorcism could require correct responses about:

- Evelyn's name
- How she died
- The person responsible
- The anchor object

This makes the investigation mechanically important.

---

## 33. Final Manifestation

The spirit reaches maximum aggression.

The player must complete the final stage of the ritual.

Possible gameplay:

- Relight extinguished candles
- Prevent ritual objects from being destroyed
- Stay inside the protective circle
- Recite sections of the ritual
- Resist supernatural attacks

This should be the most intense section of Level 1.

---

## 34. Destroy or Release the Anchor

The ritual reaches its climax.

The player must release Evelyn from the object binding her.

Depending on the object, this may involve:

- Burning it
- Breaking it
- Burying it
- Removing a binding symbol
- Performing a final ritual action

The player completes the action.

---

## 35. Silence

Everything stops instantly.

No music.

No screaming.

No movement.

The room returns to normal.

The sudden silence should feel more unsettling than another loud scare.

---

## 36. Evelyn's Final Appearance

Evelyn appears one final time.

She is no longer distorted or aggressive.

She may appear:

- In the doorway
- At the end of the hallway
- Beside the stairs

She silently looks at the player.

Then disappears.

The player is left uncertain whether Evelyn herself was truly evil or whether the years of being trapped had corrupted her.

---

## 37. Leaving the House

The player walks downstairs.

The house is completely calm.

The front door, which previously would not open, now opens immediately.

---

## 38. Returning to the Front Yard

The player steps onto the porch and crosses the front yard.

The environment is quiet.

Possible atmosphere:

- Early dawn
- Fading storm
- Quiet night
- Birds beginning to call

It appears that the case is over.

---

## 39. Final Horror Sting

As the player reaches the edge of the property:

They hear something behind them.

The player looks back at the house.

Possible ending:

- A figure stands in an upstairs window.
- The fireplace suddenly lights itself.
- Another silhouette appears beside Evelyn's former room.
- The barn door slowly opens.
- A different figure watches from inside the house.

This suggests that Evelyn may not have been the only supernatural presence connected to the property.

---

## 40. Level Complete

The journal marks Evelyn's case as closed.

However, the final event suggests that the father's occult experiments were connected to something larger.

This provides the narrative bridge into **Level 2**.

---

# Location Progression

The intended level progression is:

**Front Yard → Front Porch → Fireplace Room → Dining Room → Kitchen → Upstairs → Small Bedroom → Bathroom → Main Bedroom → Closet → Hidden Square Room → Kitchen → Backyard → Road Event → Barn/Shed → Backyard Chase → House → Fireplace → Large Empty Room → Exorcism → Front Yard**

---

# How Each Location Changes Meaning

## Front Yard

**Beginning:** Safe introduction area  
**Ending:** Exit route after surviving the haunting

## Fireplace Room

**Beginning:** First investigation area  
**Middle:** Paranormal activity centre  
**Later:** Reveals hidden evidence about Evelyn's death

## Dining Room

Provides early family history and establishes contradictions in the official story.

## Kitchen

**Beginning:** Investigation area with inaccessible back door  
**Later:** Becomes the route into the backyard

## Small Bedroom

Establishes Evelyn as a real person rather than simply "the ghost."

## Bathroom

Provides one of the first direct supernatural messages.

## Main Bedroom

Reveals the father's obsession and introduces the occult side of the mystery.

## Closet

Acts as the discovery point for the hidden square room.

## Small Square Room

**Beginning:** Strange architectural space  
**Later:** Revealed as Evelyn's prison  
**Story function:** Major emotional and narrative reveal

## Backyard

**First visit:** Investigation and transition area  
**Second visit:** Chase environment

## Road

Provides atmosphere, boundary reinforcement, and one major supernatural scare.

## Barn/Shed

**Beginning:** Inaccessible environmental landmark  
**Middle:** Major investigation site  
**Later:** Location where the anchor is discovered  
**Final use:** Starting point of the first chase

## Large Empty Room

**Beginning:** Apparently useless, unsettling space  
**Ending:** Final exorcism arena

# Core Design Rule

The horror should not rely entirely on jumpscares.

Every major supernatural event should do at least one of the following:

1. Reveal information.
2. Direct the player toward something.
3. Change how the player understands an earlier location.
4. Increase the threat level.
5. Support the central mystery.

The level should avoid becoming:

**Key → Locked Door → Jumpscare → Key → Monster → Exorcism**

Instead, the investigation itself should drive the player's movement through the environment.

---

# Narrative Information Required for the Exorcism

Before the final ritual, the player must know:

1. **Who the spirit is.**
2. **What happened to the spirit.**
3. **Who was responsible.**
4. **What object is anchoring the spirit to the property.**

The final exorcism should use this information so that exploring and understanding the story directly affects gameplay.
